Hello, I am George, 👋 🙂
a banker turned product manager. In five years of retail banking, I watched clients and staff lose time to workarounds. Now I build the tools that effectively close that gap.

My product philosophy

Be curious. Over my career, I have learned to understand the assumptions behind a request before I suggest anything. I am curious by nature, and that helps me spot tangles early and untangle them one layer at a time.

Keep it simple. I bridge the technical side of a product and what users actually understand. I build products that are easy to follow and simple to navigate, and I cut what adds clutter without adding value. At Kittii, I mapped the user journeys for both sides of the marketplace so the experience stayed seamless.

Iterate. Markets change and so do user needs; what I build today will need to adapt to stay relevant. I welcome change because it drives innovation and encourages creativity.
