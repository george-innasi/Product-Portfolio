Hello, I am George 👋
A former banker turned Product Manager. With 5 years of retail banking experience, I saw firsthand how clumsy processes waste user and staff time. Today, I build customer-centric products that bridge that gap.

My product philosophy

Discovery first. Five years in retail banking working closely with hundreds of customers taught me to look beyond surface-level requests and uncover root problems. I've built a habit of listening patiently and asking the right follow-up questions to find what truly matters.

Hands-on builder. I ship the thing. Over the past few years, I've translated problems I noticed in the field into real-world prototypes. I've recently built my first 0-to-1 product and am currently working on shipping it for real-world testing.

Evidence over instinct. Banking taught me never to rely solely on assumptions. Just as credit behavior reveals the true story behind an application, data and user actions reveal what a product needs. I systematically validate hypotheses by asking: Why this solution? Why now? Why us?
