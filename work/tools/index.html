Tools I have built

Four calculators for Canadian savings, mortgage, GIC and TFSA questions. Each one is live and free to use.

The calculators

Across five years in Canadian retail banking, similar client questions surfaced time and again. We advisors typically answered them by working through internal tools and running the numbers by traditional means, which left little room to show the client what the figures meant. I built these calculators to give advisors and clients alike a clear, visual way to walk through the answer and see how the math works.
