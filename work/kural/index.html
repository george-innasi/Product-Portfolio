Origin

How it started

The brief. Stop deconstructing products and start building one: pick a product I use often, find one real friction, and prototype an AI fix.

The friction. An AI assistant hands me a clean draft and I rewrite it anyway. The sentences are correct but not mine, and they read the same to a customer as to my manager.

The gap. Between a polished draft and a sendable one. That gap became the project.

What I built. Kural classifies who you are writing to and why, holds a persistent model of your voice, and drafts something you can send without a rewrite. I built it solo and shipped it at kural-iota.vercel.app.

The assignment

Design and prototype an AI-powered feature

The ask. Act as the PM of a product I use often and find one meaningful opportunity to improve it with AI.

My scope. One P0 problem and a demonstrable prototype.

The requirement. Validate the thinking with PMs from that industry, outside the assignment.

Meet Kural

The product

Product architecture

  1. 01_INPUT

    You give the gist.

    Rough thoughts, in your own words.

  2. 02_CLASSIFY

    It reads the situation.

    Urgency, power dynamic and emotional register are classified before a single line is written.

  3. 03_GENERATE

    It drafts in your voice.

    One pass, no "I hope this email finds you well", no padding.

  4. 04_USER_CONTROL

    You stay in control.

    Kural shows what it detected, and you adjust rather than regenerate.

How Kural Reads the Situation Before It Writes

The engine

Classify first, compose second

Tone is a decision, not an accident. The schema settles the situation before a single line is drafted.

  1. 1Classify

    Classify first

    Urgency, power and emotion are set before any draft.

  2. 2Adapt

    Adapt to the reader

    Upward, customer or peer, each reader pulls its own register.

  3. 3Draft

    Draft in your voice

    An anti-cliche guard strips stock AI openers before they ship.

  4. 4Learn

    Learn from your edits

    Style memory learns from your changes, so later drafts sound more like you.
