Lumi Cards

Instant, cited answers on credit card policy

lumi-for-cards.vercel.app

The live prototype: ask a credit card policy question. Open in a new tab ↗

Problem statement

Personal bankers and tellers in retail bank branches struggle to answer clients' credit card policy questions accurately mid-appointment, because the answers sit in dense PDFs, SharePoint folders, and disclosure tables. Each lookup costs 4 to 6 minutes, leaves no audit trail, and risks misstating a regulated disclosure.

Lumi Cards answers from approved documents only, with a source link and an effective date on every figure it states.

Who grades. Credit card subject matter experts grade in two phases, moving from manual review to an automated judge as the evidence accumulates.
1HITL calibration

Compliance officers and senior bankers score an initial sample of traces against a rubric for accuracy, completeness and citation correctness.

2Continuous LLM judge

After 100 to 200 reviewed traces become an evaluation SOP, an automated LLM judge takes over continuous scoring and regression, supported by sampled expert audits.

The set is structural at this stage. Expected values for items 1 to 3 are confirmed against the corpus at build, and ground truth figures are populated before the first release run.

Guardrails

Four refusal states, each decided before the model writes

Refusal is the designated outcome in every state. Partial answers with qualifying caveats are not permitted.

1 · Client data

Blocked before anything else

The data screen stops account numbers and SINs before embedding, retrieval, generation or logging.

2 · Out of scope

Redirect, not a dead end

The intent classifier blocks non card products with zero vector searches and links the right library.

3 · Ambiguous

One question, then retrieval

When card or province is missing and the answer varies by either, Lumi asks once before searching.

4 · Low confidence

No answer, human handoff

Below the reranker threshold, generation is suppressed and escalation to Internal Branch Support is offered.

Post-generation validation

Programmatic citation check and numeric grounding verification block any unverified token before render. A failed answer is routed to escalation.

Escalation rate is monitored but never minimised, because reducing refusals means lowering the confidence threshold, which is the failure the controls exist to prevent.

Where the cost sits

The cost is not in running the model: Index servers vs. token consumption

Index servers

The search index sits on servers that stay switched on, costing roughly two orders of magnitude more than the entire token bill.

Provisioning

The real engineering lever is how that index is provisioned, keeping it to one pinned region and minimizing active nodes.

Running the spec

Prototype tested against the requirements

Built in Google AI Studio on Gemini 2.5 Flash at temperature 0.0, against a nine document knowledge base.

The most informative pass was a refusal. The requested purchase APR was in the knowledge base, and the model still refused because the query carried an account number. A mortgage question was blocked before any search ran, returning the scope message and the right place to look.

Defect 1

Word cap and citations

The happy path hit the 120 word cap once citation text counted, because my spec never said whether it did. Amended to "excluding citation text".

Defect 2

Citation format

The model added a document version to each citation. That beat my spec, so the spec now requires the version field.

Reflection

Assessment feedback

What the assessment flagged, and what I took away

Sourcing

Price dates carried no publisher attribution. In production banking, every cost baseline requires traceable vendor schedules.

Index provisioning

Index compute was identified as the 100x cost driver but lacked explicit regional node sizing. Sized index nodes for single-region Canadian residency.

Confidence threshold

Confidence thresholding was specified conceptually across three sections. Bound the cut-off to a concrete 0.85 cosine similarity score before routing to human branch support.

Scope and disclaimer. A large Canadian bank is used as a worked example. Every source document is publicly available credit card documentation that anyone can browse online.
