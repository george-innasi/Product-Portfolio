Over five years in the financial industry I kept answering the same questions and working out the same numbers by hand. These tools turn those counter-side conversations into something anyone can see and explore for themselves.

Everyday banking

Savings Promo Calculator

Shows exactly what a customer earns by parking money at a promotional rate over a custom term or a standard 30, 60 or 90 days. Daily accrual, paid and compounded monthly, with the precise maturity date and a plain-English summary.

Purpose: give an accurate, on-the-spot answer instead of a rough mental estimate.

Lending

Visual Mortgage Calculator

Reframes mortgage paydown as building a house, where each dollar of equity colours in part of a home. Shows how rapid payments, bigger payments and lump sums get you mortgage-free sooner, using correct Canadian mortgage math.

Purpose: help homeowners feel the impact of prepaying, one of the questions I fielded most often.

Open the calculator →

Investments

GIC Calculator

Confirms the exact maturity date and applies the right Canadian rules: short-term GICs as simple interest paid at maturity, long-term GICs compounded or paid out monthly or annually.

Purpose: capture the GIC rules most online calculators get wrong, and confirm the precise day a term ends.

Open the calculator →

TFSA Room Calculator

Estimates TFSA contribution room for first-timers, newcomers and existing holders. Applies the CRA rules, where room starts the later of 2009, the year you turned 18, or the year you became a resident, with no retroactive room, and flags non-resident, no-SIN and over-contribution cases.

Purpose: I have opened hundreds of TFSAs and calculated first-timers' room by hand. This gives anyone a quick sense of their room in seconds.
