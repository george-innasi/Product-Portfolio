Background

I am an AI-enabled product manager with over five years of experience in retail banking at the three largest banks in Canada.

What I enjoyed most in banking was solving customer problems: understanding what someone actually needed, asking the right questions, and offering a solution built around them. As a banker, I helped hundreds of people through one-on-one conversations. Product management showed me a way to help thousands more.

In 2025 I built Kittii, an AI-powered lost-and-found platform, with a few friends and two interns. We took it as far as an MVP release. Building something from scratch with self-serve tools and AI let me discover, validate and build all at once. That is where I found myself pulled toward product discovery: figuring out how to create something people genuinely need.

My goal is to build products that solve real user problems and create meaningful impact, with business value at scale.

Why product management

  • Understanding people and solving meaningful problems, discovered through years of working directly with customers.
  • Building Kittii showed how much I enjoy product work, from user research and discovery to shaping solutions and experiences.
  • Product management sits at the intersection of people, business, technology, creativity and continuous learning.
